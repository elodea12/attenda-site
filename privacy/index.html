Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Attenda helps university students record class attendance and keep track of how much absence allowance they have left. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short: your courses and attendance records stay on your device. Attenda has no accounts and no analytics. The one time information leaves your phone is when you choose to import a timetable from a screenshot — that image is sent for automated reading, and is described in detail below.

Information stored on your device

The following is created and stored locally on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • Courses, course codes, names and colours
  • Weekly schedules and generated class sessions
  • Attendance records
  • Manual absence adjustments
  • App settings, such as language, appearance and reminder preferences

This information is not uploaded to Attenda, and it is not shared with anyone. It stays in the app unless you choose to send a screenshot for timetable import, as described below.

No user account

Attenda has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. You do not create an Attenda account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, student number or university.

Behind the scenes, the app signs in to our backend anonymously so that our timetable import service can tell one device apart from another and apply usage limits. This creates an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your identity, is not visible in the app, and is not used to build any profile of you.

Timetable import from a screenshot

Importing a timetable from a screenshot is optional. You can always enter your timetable by hand instead.

If you choose to import one, the image you select is sent over the internet to Attenda's backend, which passes it to an artificial-intelligence service that reads the timetable grid and returns the course codes, days and times it finds. You then review and correct the result before anything is saved.

The image is used only to provide this feature. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Attenda's own backend processes the image in memory and does not save it to any database or file storage. Once a response is returned, our backend no longer holds the image.

The artificial-intelligence service that reads the image is provided by Anthropic, and the image is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy practices as a service provider to Attenda. We do not control, and therefore do not state here, how long Anthropic retains material submitted for processing.

Please avoid submitting screenshots that contain information you do not want read by an automated service. A university timetable normally contains course codes and times.

Usage limits

To keep the timetable import service available and to prevent abuse, our backend records how many import requests have been made by an anonymous identifier on a given day. This record contains only that identifier, a date and a count. It contains no timetable content and no personal details.

Service announcements

The app periodically checks Attenda's backend for the current academic term dates, class-free days, and short service announcements. These requests ask only for that shared information. They do not send your courses, your attendance records or anything else about you, and the announcements you receive are the same for everyone — they are not targeted or personalised.

Notifications

Attenda can remind you after a class to record whether you signed the attendance sheet. Notifications are optional, and the app asks for your permission before scheduling any.

These reminders are generated and scheduled on your device. Attenda does not operate a push-notification server, and reminder content is not sent anywhere.

You can turn reminders off at any time in Attenda's settings, or turn notifications off entirely for Attenda in your device's system settings.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

Attenda contains no analytics tools, no advertising software, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app does not collect an advertising identifier or a device identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Service providers

Attenda uses these providers, and only for the purposes described above:

  • Supabase — backend infrastructure that hosts the timetable import service, the anonymous sign-in used to protect it, and the shared academic calendar and announcements.
  • Anthropic — the artificial-intelligence service that reads a timetable screenshot when you choose to import one.

Security

Information sent between the app and our backend is transmitted over encrypted connections using standard secure network protocols. Access to the data stored on our backend is restricted by database-level access rules. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Children

Attenda is intended for university students. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Timetable import from a screenshot is optional — you can enter your timetable manually.
  • Notifications are optional and can be turned off in Attenda or in your device settings.
  • Deleting the app removes the courses, attendance records and settings stored on your device. This cannot be undone, and Attenda keeps no copy to restore.

Not affiliated with any university

Attenda is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official application of any university or educational institution. Attendance records you keep in Attenda are for your own reference and are not official records.
