Attenda Support

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Getting started

Attenda keeps track of the classes you attend and how much absence allowance you have left in each course.

  • Add your courses, either by importing a timetable screenshot or by entering them by hand.
  • Set how your instructor takes attendance for each course — once per class, once every class hour, or once per day.
  • Check the suggested absence allowance and adjust it to match your course's real rule.
  • After each class, record whether you signed the attendance sheet.

Timetable import

You can set up your courses in two ways.

  • From a screenshot. Take a screenshot of your weekly timetable, choose it in Attenda, and the app reads the grid for you. This needs an internet connection.
  • By hand. Add each course with its days, times and room. This works entirely offline and is always available.

Always check the result before saving. Automated reading can misread a blurry, cropped or unusual timetable. Attenda shows you everything it found so you can fix it first — nothing is saved until you confirm.

Rooms are not read from screenshots, because timetable images do not carry that information reliably. You can type a room in yourself when adding or editing a course.

Attendance tracking

  • Attenda counts absence in class hours, not in classes, so a three-hour block missed counts as three.
  • How much one missed check costs depends on the attendance method you chose for the course. You can change that per course, or for a single class if one day was unusual.
  • Not recorded is not the same as absent. A class you have not answered for yet is flagged for review and counts nothing against you.
  • Attendance not taken is a valid answer and also counts nothing.
  • If you already had absences before installing Attenda, or your instructor corrected one, use Adjust absence count on the course screen.

Notifications

  • Attenda can remind you after a class to record whether you signed.
  • Reminders are optional. The app asks for permission before scheduling any.
  • You can choose whether a reminder arrives immediately after class or a few minutes later.
  • Reminders follow the attendance method: one per class, one per block, or one per day.
  • Everything can be changed in Attenda's settings.

Troubleshooting

Timetable import will not start, or fails.
Screenshot import needs an internet connection. If you are offline, or the service is temporarily unavailable, add your courses by hand instead — that always works.

The import limit was reached.
There is a daily limit on timetable imports to keep the service available. Try again the next day, or enter your timetable manually.

The timetable was read incorrectly.
Correct it on the review screen before saving, or edit the course afterwards. A clearer, uncropped screenshot of just the timetable grid usually reads better.

Reminders are not arriving.
Check both places. In Attenda, make sure attendance reminders are switched on in settings. In iOS Settings, open Notifications, find Attenda, and make sure notifications are allowed. If you declined the permission earlier, iOS will not ask again, so it has to be turned on in iOS Settings.

The numbers do not match my instructor's.
Check the attendance method set for the course and the absence allowance. The suggested allowance is only a starting point based on a percentage rule — your course may differ, and you can set it to whatever your instructor actually applies.

Attendance is missing after reinstalling.
Attendance records are stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes them, and Attenda keeps no copy that could restore them.

Attenda is not an official university app

Attenda is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of any university. What you record in Attenda is for your own reference — it is not an official attendance record, and your university's own records are what count.
